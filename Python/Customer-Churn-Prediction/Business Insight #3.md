


## Key Insights

Business Insight #3

Looking at the chart:

Customers paying around £70–£100 per month show much higher churn.
Customers paying lower monthly charges tend to stay longer.
High monthly charges appear correlated with customer attrition.
Business Recommendation

Review pricing plans for high-paying customers and provide loyalty offers, discounts, or premium benefits to reduce churn among high-value customers.

This is exactly the type of business recommendation interviewers want to hear.
