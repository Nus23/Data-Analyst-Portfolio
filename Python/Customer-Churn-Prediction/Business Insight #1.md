Business Insight #1

Month-to-month customers have by far the highest churn.

1,655 customers left.
One-year contracts lost only 166 customers.
Two-year contracts lost only 48 customers.
Business Recommendation

Customers on longer contracts are significantly less likely to leave. The company should encourage month-to-month customers to move to annual contracts through discounts, loyalty rewards, or bundled services.

This is exactly the kind of insight you would write in a business report.
