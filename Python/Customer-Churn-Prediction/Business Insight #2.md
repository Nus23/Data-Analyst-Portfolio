Business Insight #2

Looking at your chart:

Electronic Check customers have the highest churn.
Automatic payment methods (Bank Transfer and Credit Card) have much lower churn.

This suggests:

Customers using Electronic Check are significantly more likely to leave compared to customers using automated payment methods.

Business Recommendation

A telecom company could:

Encourage automatic payments.
Offer discounts for Direct Debit.
Provide loyalty rewards for automated payment customers.

This reduces churn and improves customer retention.
