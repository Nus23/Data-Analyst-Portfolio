Business Insight #4: Tenure is a Major Churn Driver

From your chart:

Customers with very low tenure (0-12 months) have the highest churn.
Customers who stay longer are much less likely to leave.
Customers with 60-72 months tenure rarely churn.
Business Recommendation

The company should focus retention efforts on new customers during their first year. Welcome offers, onboarding support, and loyalty incentives could significantly reduce early customer churn.
